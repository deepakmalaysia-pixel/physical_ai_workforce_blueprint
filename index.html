⚠️ IMPORTANT — PROTOTYPE & DEVELOPMENT DISCLAIMER

This website is a prototype and is still under development. We are actively seeking feedback from engineering students, graduates, educators, industry professionals and employers to improve the content, structure and usefulness of this platform. Your reactions and suggestions will help us fine-tune it.

Physical AI, robotics and their applications in robot management, deployment, training, operation and maintenance are rapidly evolving fields. The Physical AI and robotics industry in India is still at a relatively nascent stage, and the technologies, job roles, skills and employment opportunities are likely to evolve significantly over the coming years.

We believe that as the adoption of robots and intelligent machines expands across manufacturing, mobility, logistics, infrastructure, healthcare, agriculture, defence and other sectors, engineers and technically skilled professionals with the right combination of AI, robotics, electronics, software and mechanical skills will be needed in large numbers in the years ahead.

This platform is intended to help students and young professionals understand emerging opportunities, identify relevant skills and learning pathways, and prepare themselves for jobs that may emerge as the industry develops.

The information presented here is a work in progress and should not be considered a definitive or exhaustive representation of the industry or current employment opportunities.

FLAGSHIP USE CASE

Never send a person into a dangerous space first.

Use a sensor/robot to assess the atmosphere and inspect the environment before a worker enters. Sewer and manhole inspection is one example; the same principle extends to tanks, plants, mines and other confined or hazardous environments.

Gas + camera→Risk assessment→Robot inspection→Evidence + corrective action
